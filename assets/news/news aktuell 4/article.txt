[TITLE]
CERTINA Group to acquire AUMOVIO’s washer and cleaning business

[TEXT]
The CERTINA Group has signed an agreement to acquire AUMOVIO’s washer and cleaning business.

Headquartered in Adršpach, Czech Republic, the business employs around 700 people and develops cleaning systems for windshields, headlamps, cameras and optical sensors for leading OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers.

The business will join CERTINA’s automotive platform, which generates annual revenues of approximately €600 million. It complements PAL Wiping Systems, CERTINA’s Czech-based manufacturer of wiper motors and systems. The technical proximity of wiping and washing creates opportunities for joint development and a broader systems offering.

The transaction includes production, research and development, all employees and all business activities. Closing is expected by the end of 2026, subject to customary conditions.

Dr. Stefan Brungs, Head of the Automotive Division of the CERTINA Group, commented: "The business is a perfect fit for our automotive division. With its strong team in Adrspach, its proven technology and its long-standing customer relationships, the business has everything it needs to grow as an independent company. As a family-owned group with a long-term ownership model, we offer employees and customers what matters most in this transition: continuity, stability and a clear commitment to further developing the business."

The CERTINA Group looks forward to welcoming the team and working together to develop the business as an independent company.
